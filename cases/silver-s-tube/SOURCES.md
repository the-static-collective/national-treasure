## Silver halides / latent physical receipt

30. J. F. Hamilton, **The Silver Halide Photographic Process**, *Advances in Physics* 37(4) (1988), 359–441.  
    https://doi.org/10.1080/00018738800101399

31. T. Tani, **Physics of the Photographic Latent Image**, *Physics Today* 42(9) (1989).  
    https://doi.org/10.1063/1.881181

**Use:** absorption of a small number of photons can produce tiny metallic-silver latent-image clusters on silver-halide grains; those clusters can catalyze much larger reduction during development.  
**Boundary:** this is a physical latent-state/amplification mechanism, not evidence for generalized memory outside the photographic system.

## Source-pressure notes

- Several claims in this case were first discovered through modern reviews; where mechanism is load-bearing, follow citations backward to primary experiments.
- Patent prose documents Tesla's design claims and intended mechanisms; it does not automatically validate quantitative performance.
- Mathematical braid results are stronger than historical/symbolic analogies because their carrier and transform class are explicit.
- For colloidal silver, always report solvent, ionic strength, ligand/capping chemistry, size distribution, shape, concentration, temperature, and measurement method when available. `silver colloid` alone is insufficient constitution.
