# Sources and Research Leads

This file distinguishes source families by what they can support. It is not a claim that every source has already been exhaustively inspected in this case.

## Historical alchemy / Newton

### Science History Institute
Institutional background on alchemy as material practice, laboratory work, metals/minerals, heat, distillation, and the historical relation between alchemy and chemistry.

- https://www.sciencehistory.org/

Use for broad historical orientation; pin exact article/section before quoting.

### The Chymistry of Isaac Newton
Indiana University digital project preserving Newton's alchemical/chymical manuscripts, transcriptions, and research apparatus.

- https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/
- https://webapp1.dlib.indiana.edu/newton/

Use for page/manuscript-level ALEX work on Newton's vocabulary, symbols, operations, and decknames.

## Controls

### Chemistry vs nuclear transmutation
Any literal elemental-transmutation claim must be checked against modern atomic/nuclear physics. Ordinary chemical reactions rearrange electrons/bonds and do not change atomic number.
