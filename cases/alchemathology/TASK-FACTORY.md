## Promotion gates

A candidate may move from National Treasure into a runtime/spec only when:

1. the mathematical object is typed;
2. the decoder/boundary is declared;
3. a control or ablation can make the effect disappear;
4. evidence and formation motive remain separate;
5. no historical ancestry is inferred from resemblance;
6. the receiving repo explicitly owns the admission decision.
