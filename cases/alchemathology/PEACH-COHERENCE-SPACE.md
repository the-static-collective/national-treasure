# The Peach — Coherence Space

The Peach is the thread's configuration-space specimen.

## User seed

A crude glyph/frame of five `5`-like carriers was imagined across dimensional cuts:

- 2D: projection / trace;
- 3D: one coherent spatial arrangement;
- 4D: that arrangement can move;
- “5D”: the completely filled space of every place the bars could lawfully occupy while remaining one coherent framework.

The final phrase is translated here into standard modeling language rather than literal physical dimensionality.

## Formal model

Let `G` be a constrained carrier framework and let:

\[
\mathcal C
\]

be its configuration space: all embeddings/configurations satisfying the declared coherence constraints.

One realization:

\[
q\in\mathcal C.
\]

Physical occupancy of that realization:

\[
F(q)\subset\mathbb R^3.
\]

A history:

\[
q:T\to\mathcal C.
\]

A parameterized family of histories:

\[
q_s:T\to\mathcal C,
\qquad s\in S.
\]

The lawful occupancy hull is:

\[
\boxed{
\Omega=
\bigcup_{s,t}F(q_s(t)).
}
\]

`Omega` may be much larger than any one instantaneous occupancy.

## Typed ladder

```text
3D grammar:

higher-order grammar:

```

The labels “3D/4D/5D” are mnemonic in this case. The rigorous object is a configuration space plus path/family parameters.

## Projection-blind cause

Suppose:

\[
K:S\to S
\]

changes which lawful history is selected.

A lower-dimensional observer sees only:

\[
\pi_3(H_s,t)=F(q_s(t)).
\]

Then a change may be visible after projection even when the selecting coordinate `s` is absent from the projected model.

**Keeper:**

> A consequence may be visible in a projection while the variable responsible for selecting that history has been projected away.

**Control:**

This licenses the conclusion “the projected model may be causally incomplete.” It does not license “a literal fifth-dimensional being intervened.”

## Actual / reachable / forbidden

At one cut, distinguish:

1. **actual** — occupied now;
2. **reachable** — unoccupied now but reachable under lawful coherent evolution;
3. **forbidden** — violates the coherence constraints.

Thus:

```text
empty
!= impossible
```

An empty region can be part of the object's lawful possibility-space.

## Formation-of-formation

Let:

\[
\mathfrak F(X)
\]

be the space of lawful formations of `X`.

Then:

\[
X
\to
\mathfrak F(X)
\to
\mathfrak F^2(X)
\to \cdots
\]

is a typed recursion.

Required non-collapse:

\[
X\neq\mathfrak F(X)\neq\mathfrak F^2(X).
\]
