# Claim ledger — Social Construction of Authority

Use the two independent axes in [National Treasure METHOD](../../METHOD.md). Dates, claims of intent, reception, institutional capacity, and outcomes must not be silently collapsed. "Established" below refers only to the exact *narrow* wording of a claim.

## NT-SCA-001 — Shabaka's stone presents a preservation claim

```yaml
claim_id: NT-SCA-001
claim: "The surviving Shabaka Stone inscription claims to reproduce a deteriorating earlier text."
provenance_class: primary
support_confidence: established
source_family: surviving Egyptian monument / British Museum EA498
source_locator: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA498
chronology: surviving monument c. 710 BCE; alleged earlier exemplar not independently dated
reasoning: the preservation claim belongs to the monument's own text
counterevidence: the Museum calls the asserted rescue narrative possibly fictional; the old exemplar has not been independently identified
open_questions: how did identifiable historical audiences interpret the preservation claim?
downstream_relevance: claimed antiquity must be distinguished from independently attested antiquity
```

## NT-SCA-002 — A specific motive for Shabaka remains a hypothesis

```yaml
claim_id: NT-SCA-002
claim: "Shabaka commissioned the stone specifically to manipulate the Memphis population into accepting his rule."
provenance_class: speculation
support_confidence: unsupported
source_family: modern reconstruction of royal intentions
source_locator: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA498
chronology: c. 710 BCE
reasoning: the monument may be interpreted in the political and religious setting of his reign
counterevidence: commissioning a religious monument does not itself prove deception, psychological intent or measured persuasion
open_questions: what direct administrative or reception evidence could distinguish legitimation, devotion, commemoration and deception?
downstream_relevance: forbids reading intention straight off an artifact
```

## NT-SCA-008 — An authority feedback loop is not yet universally demonstrated

```yaml
claim_id: NT-SCA-008
claim: "Every publicly repeated symbol inevitably accumulates enforceable authority over its audience."
provenance_class: speculation
support_confidence: unsupported
source_family: proposed cross-case mechanism, no universal dataset
source_locator: ./MECHANISM-ATLAS.md
chronology: multiple non-equivalent historical periods
reasoning: some symbols are involved in documented institutional recognition; inevitability does not follow
counterevidence: ignored, resisted, forgotten, contested, parodic and purely decorative signs are possible and should be actively sampled
open_questions: which bounded combinations of recognition, institution, action and consequence can actually be verified?
downstream_relevance: prevents analogy from becoming historical determinism
```

## Anti-promotions

- A mark resembles a later symbol -> **not** a demonstrated transmission route.
- A text calls itself ancient -> **not** independent proof of its claimed age.
- An institution issues a decree -> **not** evidence that every subject complied.
- A credential is displayed -> **not** proof that an act was authorized.
- A biblical text is reused -> **not** transfer of its original office or authority.
- A population later changes behavior -> **not** automatically caused by the representation without comparative evidence.
