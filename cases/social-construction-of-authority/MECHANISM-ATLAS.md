# Mechanism atlas — recognition is not execution

This is a typed comparison tool, **not** a single hidden historical chain or an instruction manual for manipulating people. Use it for historical research, media literacy, legitimate institutional design and detecting impersonated authority.

## Distinct relation types

| Edge | Required historical receipt | What it does NOT imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CLAIMS_ANCESTRY` | Source object's actual statement of descent/age | ancestry is true |
| `DEMONSTRATED_TRANSMISSION` | dated copying, translation, citation or identifiable source route | unchanged meaning or institutional authority |
| `ISSUED_BY` | accountable issuer attached to a specific object | statement is true or morally legitimate |
| `RECOGNIZED_BY` | identifiable person's/group's attested acceptance, bounded in time/context | unanimous or perpetual recognition |
| `AUTHORIZED_IN` | a specific contextual rule, office, mandate or independently verifiable delegation | unrestricted powers |
| `EXERCISED_BY` | documented action by a specified actor | legitimacy of that act |
| `CONSEQUENCE_OBSERVED` | trace of material or social change in a defined population/time | representation was the only cause |
| `FORMAL_RESEMBLANCE` | described shared geometry/word pattern | any historical link |
| `CONTESTED_BY` | dated objection, refusal or alternative account | claim refuted solely by being contested |

Keep actors, documents, institutions, communities, dates, translations and observed actions as separate nodes. No generic `SAME_AS` or `RELATED_TO` relation may replace a typed edge.

## Five bounded mechanisms to probe

**M1 — Antiquity claim / custodial legitimacy.** In an ancient monument, a commissioner claims to preserve an older writing. Check the *surviving text's claim*, independent evidence for its exemplar, and evidence of contemporary recognition separately. Shabaka Stone is the starting specimen; its political effect remains unresolved.

**M2 — Cross-language institutional carry.** A decree is expressed in multiple writing systems and placed in institutional contexts. Check issuer, translation differences, actual copies, circulation, named readers, actions. Rosetta Stone is an attested text-and-distribution specimen; equality of every implication is not presumed.

**M3 — Credential and access boundary.** A material key, title or seal may signify access or identity; whether an action is allowed depends on contextually recognized authority. Isaiah 22 is a literary/administrative image; a Mesopotamian seal is a separate material-administrative specimen. Similar functions do not prove genealogy.

**M4 — Official historical narrative.** A ruler commissions a durable public account of a contested transition. Behistun attests Darius's presentation of his reign; assess events via independent records and reception via actual later evidence rather than equating proclamation with belief.

**M5 — Later interpretive reuse.** A later author or community takes up earlier authority imagery. Revelation's Davidic key reuses Isaiah's motif. This is a real textual road; the meaning and office can change, and reuse need not imply identical historical powers.

## The proposed Authority Accumulation Loop (analytical only)

```text
object / statement -> attributable issuer -> bounded recognition
-> documented behavior -> observed consequence
-> continued / contested / withdrawn recognition
```

For each arrow ask: *what specific primary record or independent observation would prove this edge?* An unfilled arrow must stay unfilled. Reverse influence is possible; an institution may create the symbol after it already has power, or coercion may produce compliance without assent.

## Rival explanations to test

- administrative convenience rather than propaganda;
- genuine shared belief or voluntary coordination rather than deception;
- coercive force or resource dependence rather than symbolic persuasion;
- independent invention or common geometry rather than transmission;
- a later re-description of prior events rather than prior intent;
- survivorship and archival bias (monuments survive; dissent may not);
- local variation and resistance rather than a homogeneous audience.

## Negative specimen rule

Preserve, at minimum, one contrasting case where a sign or decree *failed* to secure the expected recognition, one where recognition did not produce the claimed action, and one where a later form resembles an older artifact without a demonstrated transmission road. Do not claim these cases exist until sources are attached.

## Prohibited analytical shortcut

Do not infer an intentional secret organization, society-wide mental control, or supernatural mechanism merely from simultaneous use of names, numbers, geometry and religious imagery. These hypotheses require their own dated actors, primary records, routes of transmission and independently observed effects. Architectural inspiration is not historical proof.
