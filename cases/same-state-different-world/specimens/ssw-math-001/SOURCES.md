### What this source supports

Blackwell defines experiments through probability measures and compares them through attainable decision performance. He also characterizes one experiment as sufficient for another when a stochastic transformation of the first reproduces the second.

This is the conceptual ancestor of the specimen's epistemic pressure: a transformation of an existing observation does not, by itself, supply a discriminator absent from that observation.
