# PROMOTION RULE

No claim crosses from `Possible` to `Probable` because another symbolic tradition resembles it.

Promotion requires at least one of:

```text
new primary witness
new independent historical transmission evidence
predeclared statistical result
blind predictive success
physical experiment
replication
```

And any material counterexample is preserved rather than explained away.
