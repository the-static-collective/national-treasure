## Negative-space rule

A failed ancestry claim is not deleted. It remains addressable as a **refused edge**:

```text
left specimen
  -/-> claimed ancestor
reason: chronology / provenance / transmission not demonstrated
```

That refusal is useful downstream. It prevents the same attractive but unsupported bridge from being repeatedly rediscovered as if new.
