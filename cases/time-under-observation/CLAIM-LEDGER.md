## House restraint

The most important negative result is methodological:

> A structural resemblance between an old temporal idea and a modern scientific theory is **not** evidence of historical scientific knowledge unless the measurement, inference path, or transmission bridge can also be shown.

The case remains interesting after that restraint is applied. That is why it belongs here.
