# Coral Castle — Source Ledger

Accessed: 2026-08-19

This file establishes the initial source floor for the Coral Castle investigation. It intentionally favors originating texts, institutional records, site-custodian material, and directly inspectable visual evidence over interpretive retellings.

A source being admitted here does **not** mean every claim inside it is true. Admission means the source is useful, attributable, and its evidentiary role is explicit.

## Source classes

- **P — Primary artifact/text:** produced by Edward Leedskalnin or the originating artifact itself.
- **I — Institutional record:** government, archive, registry, or comparable documentary authority.
- **C — Current custodian:** official site/institution describing its own history, holdings, or interpretation.
- **W — Working witness/mirror:** useful transcription, scan mirror, or research copy that requires verification before layout-sensitive claims.
- **Q — Quarantined:** potentially useful lead, not admitted for evidentiary claims yet.

## Explicitly not admitted yet

The following classes may generate leads but cannot presently support case conclusions:

- unsourced Coral Castle conspiracy pages;
- numerology pages that do not show their count method;
- claims that Ed was a Freemason without a lodge/member record;
- “anti-gravity,” alien, acoustic, or geomagnetic-lifting claims without reproducible experimental evidence;
- screenshots whose original object/date/location cannot be established;
- modern symbolic overlays generated after seeing the target pattern.

These may be added later as **Q — Quarantined** leads with provenance intact.
