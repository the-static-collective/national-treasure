# Coral Castle

## Scope

This case studies the symbolic, architectural, textual, astronomical, and magnetic hypotheses orbiting Edward Leedskalnin, Coral Castle, and *A Book in Every Home*.

The goal is not to “solve” the site by narrative force. The goal is to preserve a disciplined map of what is observed, what is sourced, what is inferred, and what remains speculative.

See [`sources.md`](./sources.md) for the admitted source floor.

## Current working hypotheses

### CC-001 — “A Book in Every Home” points outward
**Claim:** The title *A Book in Every Home* may be intentionally pointing not only to Ed's pamphlet, but to a book already commonly present in households — most plausibly the Bible.

- **Status:** grounded speculation
- **Confidence:** C1
- **Would raise confidence:** repeated internal signals toward book/chapter/verse-style coordinates; stable textual cross-matches that were pre-registered before hunting.
- **Would lower confidence:** strong evidence the phrase was used only literally or commercially with no coordinate behavior.

### CC-002 — “Three Subjects / Sweet Sixteen” may expose `3:16`
**Claim:** The title-page phrase “Containing Three Subjects” alongside “Sweet Sixteen” may intentionally foreground the coordinate `3:16`.

- **Status:** grounded speculation
- **Confidence:** C1
- **Would raise confidence:** additional structured uses of 3 / 16; recurring 3:16 resonance across text, architecture, or pre-registered external references.
- **Would lower confidence:** evidence that the phrasing is editorially ordinary and not structurally reused anywhere else.

### CC-003 — `16` may encode complementarity / dual circuit
**Claim:** “Sweet Sixteen” may function as more than biographical language, carrying a symbolic relation to paired complementarity, polarity, or a closed dual circuit.

- **Status:** speculation
- **Confidence:** C0
- **Would raise confidence:** Ed using sixteen in structurally paired ways; repeated 8+8 / dual-loop motifs; physical objects or textual pairings tied to 16.
- **Would lower confidence:** absence of any repeated sixteen-pattern outside the phrase itself.

### CC-004 — Three `88` forms in a triangle is a candidate topology
**Claim:** A triangular arrangement of three paired-loop “88” forms may be a useful candidate topology for later comparison.

- **Status:** investigator-originated clue artifact
- **Confidence:** C0
- **Important note:** this is **not** currently claimed as an Ed symbol. It is a frozen proposal generated during investigation.
- **Would raise confidence:** later independent matches in site geometry, machinery, or comparison architecture.
- **Would lower confidence:** no meaningful convergence despite targeted searching.

### CC-005 — Coral Castle may operate as a multiple-clock instrument
**Claim:** Coral Castle may encode multiple timing systems simultaneously — solar, stellar, lunar, planetary, and possibly magnetic.

- **Status:** grounded speculation
- **Confidence:** C1
- **Would raise confidence:** independently verified astronomical alignments, seasonal indexing, and repeated cycle-sensitive design features.
- **Would lower confidence:** the apparently relevant objects prove decorative or ad hoc rather than operational.

### CC-006 — Philadelphia Masonic Temple is a comparison corpus
**Claim:** The Philadelphia Masonic Temple may be a productive external comparison object for symbolic-number and architectural grammar overlap.

- **Status:** grounded speculation
- **Confidence:** C1
- **Important note:** this is **not** evidence that Ed was a Mason or visited Philadelphia.
- **Would raise confidence:** independently verified shared numeric/formal structures or text-to-architecture grammar.
- **Would lower confidence:** claimed shared features fail counting or rely on weak secondary retellings.
