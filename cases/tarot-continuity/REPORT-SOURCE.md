# Tarot continuity — canonical report source

This file is the evidence backbone from which the readable synthesis and workbook were derived. It separates claim, support, gap, relation, and confidence so that future research can update one state without rewriting the whole story.

## Scope lock

Research question: **What kinds of continuity connect the earliest secure playing-card evidence to Tarot as a fifteenth-century Italian game, its regional game descendants, its occult and divinatory reinterpretations, and its artistic, reflective, institutional, and digital forms through 2026?**

Out of scope:

- proving or disproving supernatural causation;
- treating iconographic resemblance as ancestry without an attributable route;
- using modern practitioner testimony as a substitute for early documentary history;
- making one regional pack the universal standard;
- silently harmonizing incompatible occult correspondence systems.

## Phase chronology

### Phase 0 — card substrate, before 1410

**Documented:** East Asian paper-card traditions; Mamluk suit-card material; European ordinary-card references by 1377.  
**Inference:** European packs likely participated in a westward-moving card ecology.  
**Refusal:** none of this is yet the distinctive Tarot trump game.

### Phase 1 — distributed invention, c. 1410–1450

**Documented:** court descriptions/accounts and *trionfi* language; surviving Italian cards.  
**Inference:** permanent allegorical or exemplary trumps were added to an ordinary suit pack through multiple experiments.  
**Unresolved:** first city, first workshop, precise inventor, first order, exact relation among Martiano, Florence, Ferrara, Milan, and Bologna.

### Phase 2 — workshop standardization and mutation, c. 1450–1750

**Documented:** painted packs, printed sheets and packs, Sola Busca, later Marseille-family objects, regional variants.  
**Inference:** copying networks stabilized recognizable addresses while local makers altered form.  
**Unresolved:** many workshop dependency graphs; full geography of cheap packs lost through use.

### Phase 3 — cartomantic and occult fork, c. 1750–1900

**Documented:** Bolognese meanings, Etteilla publications/deck, Court’s 1781 Egyptian theory, nineteenth-century occult syntheses.  
**Inference:** randomization plus a symbolically dense sequence made Tarot unusually adaptable as an interpretive protocol.  
**Refusal:** later claims do not retroactively establish ancient ancestry.

### Phase 4 — mass visual grammars, 1909–1970

**Documented:** Waite–Smith publication; Thoth paintings/text/deck; artistic experiments.  
**Inference:** scenic suit cards lowered the amount of memorized doctrine required and increased narrative affordance.  
**Unresolved:** exact reception and visual-source pathways for some individual images.

### Phase 5 — plural authoring, 1970–2020

**Documented:** feminist, independent, identity-centered, therapeutic-adjacent, literary, and art decks.  
**Inference:** the deck becomes a platform whose recognizability depends on partial preservation of addresses and relations, not identical art.  
**Risk:** representation and appropriation can coexist.

### Phase 6 — platform and museum ecosystem, 2020–2026

**Documented:** living rules/federations, museum exhibitions, survey use, apps and emerging AI research.  
**Inference:** institutions and interfaces now mediate Tarot as strongly as card manufacture.  
**Unresolved:** global scale, longitudinal effects, platform incentives, and whether current AI forms persist.

## Evidence precedence

When claims conflict, this report uses the following order:

1. securely provenanced object or contemporaneous document;
2. critical edition, collection catalog, official rule set, or specialist peer-reviewed history;
3. museum and library interpretive essay, checked against its object record and specialists;
4. high-quality contemporary survey or study for current use only;
5. practitioner testimony for practice and reception, not ancient history;
6. unsourced repetition, visual analogy, or marketing copy—retained only as a claim under test.

## Tempo calculations

Wolfram Language was used only to calculate intervals between selected documentary anchors; the dates themselves come from the historical sources. Approximate gaps:

- 1377 European card record → 1440 *trionfi* record: 22,928 days, about 62.8 years;
- 1440 *trionfi* → 1781 Court de Gébelin: 124,549 days, about 341.0 years;
- 1781 Court → 1909 Waite–Smith: 47,084 days, about 128.9 years;
- 1909 Waite–Smith → 2025 Warburg exhibition: 42,065 days, about 115.2 years;
- 2025 Warburg → 2026 Morgan exhibition opening: 511 days, about 1.4 years.

The long 1440–1781 interval is the most important sanity check: the documented occult-origin story arrives more than three centuries after Tarot’s secure game record.
