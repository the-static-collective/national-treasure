# MADDCL0WN attack — Tarot continuity

The adversarial question is not “Is the story compelling?” It is “Which parts fail when prestige, chronology, representation, and attractive resemblance stop cooperating?”

## The harshest surviving objections

### “Italian invention” can still be too neat

It is the best current statement about Tarot’s distinctive trump-game architecture, but it can conceal distributed experiments and uneven archives. The dossier therefore says “northern and central Italian development, secure by the 1440s” and preserves the unresolved city/workshop graph.

### “Mamluk influence” can exceed the object record

The four-suit comparison is strong; the actual human transmission chain is thin. The dossier labels the card-substrate connection Probable, not Demonstrated, and refuses to smuggle Tarot’s trump sequence into the Mamluk deck.

### “No evidence” can be abused

Absence of a surviving record is not proof that no private practice or contact ever occurred. It is enough to withhold a positive ancestry claim. The ledger says Unsupported, not metaphysically impossible.

### “Multiple meanings” can become lazy relativism

Forked traditions do not make every statement equally supported. A reading can be locally coherent; an origin claim still needs sources. Relation type and confidence remain mandatory.

### “Living game” can become token correction

Listing countries is not a population study. Current gameplay is established, but comparative scale is unresolved. The case does not claim that game use dominates globally.

### “Representation” can hide extraction

Identity-centered decks can repair inherited exclusions and still borrow from cultures without accountable relationships. Contemporary ethical evaluation must remain edition- and community-specific.

## Surviving core

After the attacks, five claims remain strong:

1. Tarot’s distinctive documentary history begins as a fifteenth-century Italian trump game.
2. Its strongest continuous branch is the reproducible pack plus regional play.
3. Its image repertoire persists through copying and re-encoding, not through one fixed secret meaning.
4. Divinatory and occult Tarot is a documented later fork whose historical influence is large even when its ancient genealogies fail.
5. Modern Tarot is a plural media ecosystem; continuity resides in overlapping relations rather than an unchanged essence.

## Gate result

- Unsupported ancestries are retained as refused claims, not deleted.
- Unresolved invention and transmission questions remain explicit.
- Object, textual, institutional, practitioner, and survey evidence are not blended.
- Current digital claims are provisional.
- No architectural promotion is requested.
