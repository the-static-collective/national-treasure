## Explicit unresolved set

1. The precise east-to-west path by which card technologies and suit forms reached Europe.
2. The exact date, city, patron, designer, and workshop of the first pack recognizable as Tarot.
3. The dependency graph among the earliest Milanese, Ferrarese, Florentine, and Bolognese experiments.
4. The earliest full trump order and whether any one order preceded all regional lists.
5. The direct sources for each early triumph image.
6. The complete cheap-print and ordinary-player record, much of which was used up rather than preserved.
7. The exact access chain between Sola Busca imagery and Pamela Colman Smith.
8. Comparative global statistics separating Tarot play, divination, art, collection, reflection, and spiritual practice.
9. Longitudinal evidence about AI-mediated readings, platform incentives, privacy, reliance, and harm.
