## Refused promotions

These claims are **not** authorized by the present case:

```text
Yāḫû-šūʿ existed -> therefore Jesus was certainly called Yahushua

Yashi resembles Yishai -> therefore Jesus's brothers called him Yashi

David = 14 in one spelling -> therefore every ancient David text encodes 14

sevenfold Spirit imagery -> therefore one intentional mega-menorah cipher

waw/vav sits between two dalets -> therefore דוד etymologically means door-hinge-door

Revelation tracks Hebrew Isaiah -> therefore the author personally translated a Hebrew scroll

Key imagery crosses several domains -> therefore all keys in Scripture mean one identical thing
```

## Working promotion gate

A speculative edge may move upward only if at least one of the following appears:

1. a primary textual or epigraphic witness,
2. an independently dated linguistic parallel with a defensible transmission road,
3. multiple independent scholarly analyses converging on the same relation,
4. a falsifiable textual prediction that succeeds against hostile controls.
