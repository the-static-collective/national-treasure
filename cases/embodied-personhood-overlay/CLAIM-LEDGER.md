## Standing anti-overfit control

The visually compelling vertical correspondence

```text
pelvis → belly → heart → throat → brow/head
```

is **not** itself evidence of historical transmission, subtle-energy anatomy, or independent discovery of the same causal mechanism.

Future work should deliberately test body maps that do not resemble the modern chakra scheme and use preregistered matching criteria. If nearly any symbolic anatomy can be made to fit ordinary physiology after the fact, the apparent convergence should be downgraded.
