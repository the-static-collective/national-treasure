# Social Biology Layer — Source Ledger

This ledger supports [`SOCIAL-BIOLOGY.md`](./SOCIAL-BIOLOGY.md). It preserves source families separately so that mirror-system evidence, autonomic synchrony, hyperscanning, touch, stress regulation, and inflammation are not collapsed into one evidentiary stream.

## Mirror neurons / action-perception coupling

### Gallese V, Fadiga L, Fogassi L, Rizzolatti G. — *Action recognition in the premotor cortex* (1996)

- Journal: *Brain* 119(Pt 2):593–609
- PMID: 8800951
- DOI: 10.1093/brain/119.2.593
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8800951/
- Source class: **Primary source**
- Relevance: direct recording of macaque F5 neurons active during both action execution and observation; foundational mirror-neuron specimen.
- Boundary: macaque single-neuron evidence does not itself establish human empathy, intention-reading, language, autism theory, or spirituality.

### Heyes C, Catmur C. — *What Happened to Mirror Neurons?* (2022)

- Journal: *Perspectives on Psychological Science* 17(1):153–168
- PMID: 34241539
- PMCID: PMC8785302
- DOI: 10.1177/1745691621990638
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34241539/
- Source class: **Review**
- Relevance: retrospective evaluation of major mirror-neuron claims; supports action identification/imitation more strongly than high-level intention inference; notes failed support for “broken mirror” autism theory.

### Thompson EL, Bird G, Catmur C. — *Mirror neuron brain regions contribute to identifying actions, but not intentions* (2022)

- Journal: *Human Brain Mapping*
- PMID: 35906896
- DOI: 10.1002/hbm.26036
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35906896/
- Source class: **Primary experimental / convergent human evidence**
- Relevance: separates lower-level action identification from higher-level intention inference.

## Physiological attunement / interpersonal autonomic synchrony

### Bastos MAV Jr et al. — *Physiological attunement and flourishing: understanding the influence of relationships on health* (2025)

- Journal: *Frontiers in Psychiatry* 16:1614379
- PMID: 40821019
- PMCID: PMC12354601
- DOI: 10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1614379
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40821019/
- Source class: **Scoping review**
- Relevance: 62 adult dyadic studies; attunement across heart rate, HRV, skin conductance, respiration, cortisol, and alpha-amylase; emphasizes context sensitivity, co-regulation, and co-dysregulation.

### Mayo O, Lavidor M, Gordon I. — *Interpersonal autonomic nervous system synchrony and its association to relationship and performance — a systematic review and meta-analysis* (2021)

- Journal: *Physiology & Behavior* 235:113391
- PMID: 33744259
- DOI: 10.1016/j.physbeh.2021.113391
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33744259/
- Source class: **Systematic review / meta-analysis**
- Relevance: autonomic synchrony is measurable, but effect sizes for relational/performance outcomes are generally small and heterogeneous; sympathetic and parasympathetic synchrony can behave differently.

### Koul A, Novembre G. — *Interpersonal Synchronization of Autonomic Physiology via Mere Visual Contact* (2026)

- Journal: *Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences* 1560(1):e70322
- PMID: 42301140
- PMCID: PMC13271046
- DOI: 10.1111/nyas.70322
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42301140/
- Source class: **Primary experimental source**
- Relevance: 38 familiar dyads; visual contact produced above-chance synchrony in heart rate, skin conductance, respiration, and pupil diameter relative to surrogate dyads; some measures decreased when vision was blocked.
- Boundary: the study itself identifies exchange of visual/social cues; it is not evidence of non-sensory field transmission.

## Neural synchrony / hyperscanning

### Zhao Q et al. — *Interpersonal neural synchronization during social interactions in close relationships: A systematic review and meta-analysis of fNIRS hyperscanning studies* (2024)

- Journal: *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 158:105565
- PMID: 38295965
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2024.105565
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38295965/
- Source class: **Systematic review / meta-analysis**
- Relevance: synthesis of 17 fNIRS hyperscanning studies involving close relationships; reports frontal, temporal, and parietal interpersonal neural synchronization.

### Carollo A et al. — *Interpersonal neural synchrony across levels of interpersonal closeness and social interactivity* (2025)

- Journal: *NeuroImage* 322:121532
- PMID: 41110650
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2025.121532
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41110650/
- Source class: **Primary experimental source**
- Relevance: 142 dyads spanning friends, romantic partners, and mother-child pairs; true dyads exceeded surrogate dyads in synchrony, with generally small effects.

### Ohayon S, Gordon I. — *Multimodal interpersonal synchrony: Systematic review and meta-analysis* (2025)

- Journal: *Behavioural Brain Research* 480:115369
- PMID: 39653120
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbr.2024.115369
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39653120/
- Source class: **Systematic review / meta-analysis**
- Relevance: neural–behavioral synchrony showed a medium association; physiological–behavioral synchrony a smaller association; neural–physiological evidence remained sparse/inconsistent.

### Grasso-Cladera A et al. — *Embodied hyperscanning for studying social interaction: A scoping review of simultaneous brain and body measurements* (2025)

- Journal: *Social Neuroscience* 20(3):163–179
- PMID: 39387663
- DOI: 10.1080/17470919.2024.2409758
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39387663/
- Source class: **Scoping review**
- Relevance: explicitly argues for simultaneous study of brain, body, and environment in real social interaction; useful methodological anchor for the “inter-bodied” layer.

## Social buffering / stress contagion

### DeVries AC, Glasper ER, Detillion CE. — *Social modulation of stress responses* (2003)

- Journal: *Physiology & Behavior* 79(3):399–407
- PMID: 12954434
- DOI: 10.1016/S0031-9384(03)00152-5
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12954434/
- Source class: **Review**
- Relevance: social interaction can modulate HPA-axis activity; affiliative behavior/social support can buffer stress in humans and animals.

### Peen NF, Duque-Wilckens N, Trainor BC. — *Convergent neuroendocrine mechanisms of social buffering and stress contagion* (2021)

- Journal: *Hormones and Behavior* 129:104933
- PMID: 33465346
- PMCID: PMC7965339
- DOI: 10.1016/j.yhbeh.2021.104933
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33465346/
- Source class: **Review**
- Relevance: explicitly compares stress contagion and social buffering; discusses overlapping CRH, oxytocin, insular and frontal-cortical mechanisms.
- Boundary: much mechanistic evidence is animal-model based; human generalization must be bounded.

## Social touch

### Schirmer A, Croy I, Ackerley R. — *What are C-tactile afferents and how do they relate to “affective touch”?* (2023)

- Journal: *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 151:105236
- PMID: 37196923
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2023.105236
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37196923/
- Source class: **Review**
- Relevance: CT afferents support gentle/affective touch but do not exhaust affective touch and are not guaranteed to produce pleasantness.

## Social isolation / inflammation

### Matthews T et al. — *Social isolation, loneliness, and inflammation: A multi-cohort investigation in early and mid-adulthood* (2024)

- Journal: *Brain, Behavior, and Immunity* 115:727–736
- PMID: 37992788
- PMCID: PMC11194667
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbi.2023.11.022
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37992788/
- Source class: **Primary multi-cohort longitudinal/observational evidence**
- Relevance: social isolation showed associations with inflammatory markers, especially suPAR; effects varied across biomarkers and cohorts.
- Boundary: association and partial longitudinal prediction do not justify a simple one-step causal formula from loneliness to inflammation.

## Biblical textual neighbors for later lexical pass

The social-biology layer currently treats the biblical material as **interpretive neighbors**, not neuroscientific predictions. A later textual pass should preserve language/context around at least:

- Romans 12:15 — rejoicing/weeping with others;
- Galatians 6:2 — bearing burdens;
- 1 Corinthians 4:16 / 11:1 and related Pauline imitation language;
- 1 Corinthians 12 — body/members metaphor;
- communal laying-on-of-hands, touch, meals, singing, and embodied worship passages;
- companionship / corrupting-formative company traditions.

That later pass should use original-language and textual-context sources before any stronger lexical claim is promoted.

## Standing source rule

> **Synchrony is evidence of relation in time, not evidence of the channel that produced the relation.**

Any future claim of direct subtle-field or brain-to-brain transmission must survive ordinary sensory-channel controls and predict something the established social-information pathways do not.
