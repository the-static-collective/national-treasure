## E. Cardiac / neural electromagnetic and interoceptive physiology

The case distinguishes ordinary biomagnetism and electrophysiology from stronger “subtle field” claims.

Established instrument families include:

- electrocardiography (ECG);
- electroencephalography (EEG);
- magnetoencephalography (MEG);
- magnetocardiography (MCG).

Future source hardening should add authoritative instrumentation reviews rather than relying on general background knowledge.

The interoception sources above support the narrower claim that cardiac and visceral signaling participates in brain-body regulation and subjective bodily state.

## F. Ultraweak photon emission

The case preserves the established observation that living tissue produces ultraweak photon emission and explicitly refuses to identify this with clairvoyant aura perception without evidence.

A future hardening pass should add the most methodologically rigorous recent human UPE review and primary measurement studies, including calibration/background controls and wavelength/intensity ranges.

No current claim in this case depends on biophotons functioning as an organism-wide cognitive communication channel.

## G. Reiki / proposed subtle biofield

### National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH)

**Reiki.** Current evidence overview.  
https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/reiki

NCCIH’s bottom-line assessment is that Reiki has not been clearly shown effective for any health-related purpose, much of the research is low quality/inconsistent, and there is no scientific evidence supporting the proposed energy field thought to play a role in Reiki.

Use: high-authority current evidence boundary.

The case intentionally distinguishes this claim from measurable bioelectricity, ECG/EEG/MEG/MCG, and tissue electric fields.

## I. Western chakra-system reception history

### Kurt Leland. 2016.

**Rainbow Body: A History of the Western Chakra System from Blavatsky to Brennan.** Ibis Press.

The case currently accesses the historical thesis through Leland’s article for the Theosophical Society in America:

**“The Rainbow Body: How the Western Chakra System Came to Be.”** *Quest* 105.2 (Spring 2017), 25–29.  
https://www.theosophical.org/publications/quest-magazine?sa=U&start=775

The article separates the progressive Western assembly of:

- seven-chakra base;
- nerve-plexus associations;
- vernacular names;
- endocrine-gland associations;
- spectrum/rainbow colors;
- standardized psychological/spiritual qualities.

A later Quest republication summarizes the same thesis and states that the rainbow colors and psychological-quality list did not converge in print until 1977.  
https://www.theosophical.org/publications/quest-magazine?start=5

Use: reception-history lead.

**Important provenance restraint:** Theosophical Society material is highly relevant as evidence about Theosophical/Western esoteric transmission, but it is not neutral authority for physiological truth. Each historical component should eventually be checked against the primary publications Leland cites.
