## Mathal 1 — NO-NEW-STATE

The human witness called the warning-like experience **entropy**.

The cleaner dynamical analogue may be loss of available transition rather than thermodynamic entropy.

Let

```math
\operatorname{Next}(x)=\{y : x\to y\}.
```

The feared state was phenomenologically close to:

```math
|\operatorname{Next}(x)|\to 0.
```

or, in a stochastic model, an absorbing state:

```math
P_{xx}=1,
\qquad
P_{xy}=0\quad(y\neq x).
```

The important content is not "maximum disorder."

> **NO NEXT STATE.**

## New duality — forgiveness and testimony

This pass exposes a useful opposition.

### Forgiveness points forward

```text
PAST RECEIPT: preserved
FUTURE CONSTRAINT: loosened
```

Forgiveness refuses to let a past wound determine every admissible future.

### Testimony points backward / across

```text
PAST ATTRIBUTION: preserved
FUTURE CARRIER: may change
```

Testimony allows an event relation to remain addressable after the original occurrence has passed and even after carriers change.

Therefore:

> **FORGIVENESS OPENS THE FUTURE WITHOUT ERASING THE PAST.**

> **TESTIMONY PRESERVES THE PAST WITHOUT FREEZING THE FUTURE.**

Those are complementary operators.

A candidate commuting constraint is that forgiveness should not alter historical attribution and testimony should not restore a terminal relational constraint merely by preserving its receipt.
