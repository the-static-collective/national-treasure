# 3. Promotion speciation — portable laws may split

## Status

**UNSLICED.**

GitBook's current promotion threshold already says:

> Frequency is not the threshold. Survival under translation is.

It also explicitly leaves open what should happen when a promoted pattern fails under a new domain, and what evidence should **demote or split** a pattern after promotion.

The unsliced delta is to make that failure mode first-class rather than treating promotion as a one-way staircase.

## Candidate pressure

A promotion crucible should be allowed to return something conceptually like:

```text
PORTABLE
VOCABULARY LEAKAGE
REFUSED
SPLIT
```

`SPLIT` must not mean "the adversary invented two prettier names."

A valid split would require two smaller candidate descendants that explain the prior supporting specimens **and** the new counterexample better than the parent invariant does.

Conceptually:

```text
candidate invariant P
    ↓ survives domains A + B
    ↓ fails or bifurcates in domain C

         ┌→ P1
P  ──────┤
         └→ P2

P1 + P2 explain A/B/C
with fewer hidden assumptions
than P alone
```

## Why this matters

Without a split path, promotion can acquire accidental irreversibility:

```text
local observation
→ repeated recurrence
→ portable pattern
→ untouchable abstraction
```

The healthier model is phylogenetic:

```text
local ancestors
→ translated descendants
→ counterexample
→ refinement | split | death | succession
```

This composes structurally with manuscript variation and local death/succession, but those are analogical controls rather than authority for the promotion system.

## Failure modes

- **Over-splitting:** every difficult specimen can be "explained" by fragmenting a law until nothing predicts anything.
- **Post-hoc naming:** P1/P2 merely rename the observed outcomes instead of constraining future cases.
- **Vocabulary genes:** the apparent split is really one domain leaking local terminology into the supposedly portable statement.
- **Ancestor erasure:** the parent pattern is rewritten as though it was never supported.

## Graduation gate

This becomes more than an unsliced idea only if one real candidate portable pattern:

1. survives at least two materially different embodiments;
2. encounters a third specimen that breaks the parent form;
3. admits two smaller descendants that make distinct predictions;
4. preserves the parent and its earlier evidence as historical ancestry;
5. demonstrates that `SPLIT` explains the evidence better than `REFUSE`, `DEMOTE`, or simple vocabulary repair.

Until then:

> **Promotion may speciate**

is a pressure-test hypothesis, not a law.

# 4. Addressable epistemic gaps — missing witness without fictional event

## Status

**PARTLY HOUSED, DELTA UNSLICED.**

Iron Lung already carries **Addressable Absence**:

> Do not erase the hole. Give it an address.

That primitive is broader than the Full Bowl phase leak, and the original Corpus witness compression has since been repaired in Full Bowl 002.

The remaining unsliced contribution is a stricter epistemic distinction:

```text
ABSENT
    evidence establishes non-presence / non-occurrence

UNOBSERVED
    the witness surface does not establish what happened

KNOWN-NOT-TO-HAVE-OCCURRED
    positive evidence excludes the event
```

Those states must not collapse merely because all three can appear as "missing data."

## Candidate gap coordinate

For an expected but externally unreconstructible phase, a system might preserve an inert coordinate such as:

```text
phase-witness-gap:<run>:attempt
```

with only bounded metadata:

```text
expectedPhase
sourceSurface
observationCut
reasonForEpistemicAbsence
resolutionStatus
laterResolutionRef?
authorityEffect: none
```

It must **not** contain fictional event content.

A gap coordinate says:

> This is the exact proof obligation we cannot currently satisfy.

It does **not** say:

> The missing event happened.

## Why this may travel

The same smaller distinction appears plausibly useful for:

- missing consequential phases;
- missing manuscript leaves;
- redacted/sealed sources;
- unavailable sensor channels;
- unreachable evidence surfaces;
- interrupted acquisition histories.

But this cross-domain rhyme is exactly why it belongs in `unsliced` for now.

## Resolution law candidate

Later evidence should append a relation to the gap rather than rewriting the earlier epistemic state:

```text
t0: gap g existed; phase unreconstructible

t1: new witness w arrives
    g --resolved-by--> w
```

The historical statement "we could not prove this at t0" remains true after t1.

## Failure modes

- gap objects become placeholders that silently imply expected events really occurred;
- every null field gets a global identity, creating address spam;
- a resolution overwrites the old ignorance instead of preserving it;
- gap coordinates acquire authority or execution semantics;
- local domain distinctions are forced into one universal absence taxonomy too early.

## Graduation gate

Require at least two materially different systems where an addressable epistemic gap materially improves reconstruction or later resolution **without** manufacturing event content.

Until then:

> **A hole may deserve an address without deserving a story.**

## Candidate identity

One deliberately small candidate is:

```text
projectionIdentity = commitment(
  coordinate,

  decoderVersion,
  resultDigest

```

A concrete implementation might eventually call the commitment `projectionHash`, but this packet does **not** freeze that field name, hashing algorithm, serialization, canonicalization, or shared schema.

The pressure is only:

```text
same coordinate
+ same named lens
+ different decoder version
→ historically distinct projections may exist
```

while:

```text
coordinate ancestry unchanged
authority unchanged
source object unchanged
```

## Relation to Sigil Algebra

Sigil Algebra's distinction between historical expression identity and normalized mathematical value supplies a useful neighboring control:

```text
expressionHash != normalFormHash
```

Likewise, two projections may be semantically equivalent or visually identical while retaining different historical identities because their decoder ancestry differs.

That does not make the analogy proof.

## "Looking becomes an event" — restraint

The originating synthesis compressed this as:

> looking becomes an event.

Keep the phrase metaphorical.

A projection record may acquire historical identity because a deterministic decoder produced attributable bytes under a declared cut. That does not require claiming that every human act of perception becomes a system event, nor that observer consciousness is being modeled.

## Failure modes

- the hash merely duplicates an existing receipt identity;
- result bytes alone accidentally define projection identity and erase decoder ancestry;
- decoder version is treated as authority;
- equivalent output causes historical projections to be silently deduplicated;
- hashing local fields prematurely creates a shared schema before a second consumer exists.

## Graduation gate

Free Graph issue #2 remains the cheapest owner-local specimen.

This candidate earns stronger standing only if the decoder-change control demonstrates that:

1. old and new projections remain separately addressable;
2. coordinate/source ancestry remains fixed;
3. authority/access/export remain fixed;
4. identical result values do not require historical identity collapse;
5. a materially different second consumer independently needs the same separation.

Until then:

> **A new reading may descend from the same object without overwriting the old reading.**

# 6. Conservation under compilation — truth-preserving transforms without semantic promotion

## Status

**UNSLICED CROSS-DOMAIN CRUCIBLE.**

Several unrelated Collective surfaces now resemble compiler passes:

### Human Terminal

```text
rich runtime distinctions
    ↓ basic-human projection
legible operator surface
```

The vocabulary may simplify. Known distinctions may not be flattened.

### Relational Coordinate

```text
stable object / coordinate
    ↓ typed decoder lens
purpose-shaped projection
```

Legibility may change. Object identity, authority, disclosure, and consequence may not be silently manufactured.

### Compost Translation

```text
foreign research machinery
    ↓ controlled observation
behavioral invariant / public math / spec
    ↓ native regrowth
foreign runtime dependency drops out
```

The implementation may change radically. Whatever invariant justified the translation must survive or the composting claim fails.

## Candidate common shape

```text
SOURCE REPRESENTATION / BODY
        ↓
   compiler pass C
        ↓
TARGET REPRESENTATION / BODY

C must declare:
  what MUST survive
  what MAY change
  what MUST NOT be manufactured
```

The interesting possibility is not that everything is literally a compiler.

It is that trustworthy transformations may share a conservation discipline.

## Tiny crucible

Use exactly three materially different passes:

1. **Corpus/runtime dispositions → Human Terminal**
2. **Free Graph coordinate → typed projection**
3. **foreign research behavior → native Compost Translation**

For each, produce three sets:

```text
MUST_SURVIVE
MAY_CHANGE
MUST_NOT_MANUFACTURE
```

Then compare the sets.

### Example pressure only

Human Terminal might require preservation of outcome distinctions and refusal/unknown state while permitting vocabulary simplification.

Relational Coordinate might require preservation of source identity/ancestry/authority posture while permitting selected visibility and representation to change.

Compost Translation might require preservation of tested behavior/invariants while permitting implementation language, dependency graph, data shape, and internal machinery to change.

If no non-trivial common conserved set survives all three, **refuse the universal pattern**.

That negative result would be useful.

## Strong failure mode

The dangerous compiler is one that introduces semantic promotion:

```text
proposal → fact
view → authority
readability → permission
similar behavior → claimed ancestry
aggregate result → invented internal phases
missing evidence → invented event
```

A trustworthy compiler should make those promotions impossible or externally visible.

## Possible conserved minimum

Do not promote this yet, but the smallest candidate family presently looks like:

```text
provenance / source relation
known distinctions relevant to consequence
explicit loss
authority boundary
historical non-overwrite
```

That set may still be too abstract. The crucible exists to find out.

## Graduation gate

Only consider a portable **Conservation Under Compilation** pattern if:

1. all three passes can state a non-trivial conserved set;
2. at least one adversarial transform is rejected for manufacturing semantics;
3. loss is explicitly representable rather than hidden;
4. the shared set is more specific than generic "don't lie" guidance;
5. the same invariant changes a concrete design or test in at least two owners;
6. irreducibly local semantics remain local.

Otherwise keep the three compiler-like systems separate.
