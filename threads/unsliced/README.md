# Unsliced Threads

This directory is a holding pen for research fragments that are worth preserving but have **not** yet earned promotion into a constituted case, cross-case clue, portable pattern, or executable claim.

`unsliced` is a status, not a verdict.

It may contain:

- external control cases that still need source pinning or adversarial comparison;
- analogies that look structurally useful but have not survived enough pressure;
- research leads whose relevance is visible before their strongest claims are established;
- fragments awaiting a better owner elsewhere in National Treasure or the wider Static Collective.

## Boundary

Material here must remain explicitly non-promoted:

```text
interesting relation
    !=
verified ancestry
    !=
constituted evidence
    !=
portable law
    !=
execution authority
```

Do not upgrade a claim merely because it recurs, rhymes with existing architecture, or becomes useful to another project.

Where possible, preserve:

- source ancestry;
- what is documented versus inferred versus speculative;
- the specific Collective question the fragment might pressure-test;
- counterevidence and failure modes;
- what evidence would justify promotion or composting.

## Promotion

An unsliced thread may leave this directory when it has a clear destination and the receiving surface can state what was actually earned.

Promotion should create a descendant with an attributable road back here rather than silently rewriting the earlier uncertainty.

The holding pen is deliberately allowed to stay messy. Its job is to keep potentially valuable fragments from disappearing before they deserve a cleaner name.
