# Probe + Opening Bounties — 2026-08-28

Status: **BOUNTY BOARD / no findings claimed**

This file preserves research holes generated by the current ALEX / Dogram / 3rdi / LOADOUT frontier. It does not assert that any historical specimen exists, shares ancestry with Static Collective architecture, or carries symbolic significance.

The bounties are useful only if they return documented, attributable cases that survive the stated controls.

---

## BOUNTY 1 — CHOSEN QUESTION

### Target shape

Find a historically documented case where passive observation left two or more candidate explanations compatible, then a deliberately chosen challenge separated them.

```text
same / compatible observation
        ↓
predeclared intervention
        ↓
distinct response
```

Strong domains may include:

- cryptanalysis / challenge-response;
- scientific model discrimination;
- material authentication;
- mechanical/electrical diagnosis;
- instrument calibration;
- controlled chemistry or physiology.

### Required receipts

- what rival possibilities existed before the intervention;
- what intervention was selected and why;
- evidence the intervention was actually delivered;
- measured response;
- what candidates were eliminated or retained;
- what remained unresolved.

### Kill condition

Reject cases where the alleged discriminator was invented after the answer was already known.

## BOUNTY 2 — SAME SURFACE, DIFFERENT DISPOSITION

### Target shape

Find materially similar or visually indistinguishable objects/states whose internal difference became observable under one common test.

```text
same visible starting condition
+
same attributable challenge
→
different response
```

The useful relation is **disposition under a held challenge**, not visual mystery.

### Kill conditions

- challenge conditions differ materially;
- environmental conditions explain the response split;
- the example only shows correlation, not a common intervention;
- the historical record does not establish the pre-test observational collision.

## BOUNTY 3 — THE ACTIVE DECODER

### Target shape

Find historical investigative or interpretive instruments whose act of "reading" necessarily injects an input capable of changing the tested system.

Candidate families may include:

```text
light / radiation
sound / vibration
electrical excitation
chemical reagent
mechanical load / resonance
chosen plaintext / challenge input
```

Question:

> **At what point does historical reading become an intervention whose own formation belongs in the evidence path?**

### Required distinction

```text
passive observation
!=
active interrogation
```

### Kill condition

Do not call an instrument active merely because it consumes power; the measurement/input must materially interact with the target in a way relevant to the inference.

## BOUNTY 4 — THE EMPTY PLACE THAT CHANGES THE WHOLE

### Target shape

Find documented systems where maintaining an empty seat, berth, port, socket, doorway, vacancy, gap, or admission surface materially changes how new participation can occur.

```text
maintained opening
→
changes admissible participation topology
```

This bounty is the historical control for [`The Thirteenth May Be an Opening`](../../clues/the-thirteenth-may-be-an-opening.md).

### Critical refusal

Do **not** search for "13" first.

The topology must be found independently of the project's symbolic compression.

### Kill conditions

- empty place is decorative only;
- vacancy has no procedural/causal role;
- new participant could enter identically without the maintained opening;
- example is selected for numerological resemblance rather than topology.

## BOUNTY 5 — SURFACE-FIBER ARCHAEOLOGY

### Target shape

Find manuscript, inscription, craft, forensic, or archaeological cases where the same surviving surface/form is compatible with multiple production histories and responsible scholarship characterizes the compatible family rather than pretending exact reconstruction.

```text
one observed carrier
←
multiple plausible formation histories
```

Look especially for cases where some formation properties are inferable across **every** compatible history while others remain unresolved.

### Required receipts

- observed carrier;
- candidate formation family;
- invariant properties across candidates;
- discriminating evidence/probes if any;
- explicit residual uncertainty.

### Kill condition

Reject reconstructions that rank one formation only through circular use of the interpretation the formation is supposed to support.

## BOUNTY 6 — BOUNDARY SUFFICIENCY

### Target shape

Find documented technical or historical systems where a complex interior can be replaced, for a declared class of tasks, by a smaller interface/boundary description that answers those tasks exactly while refusing or deferring questions requiring interior detail.

This may appear in engineering interfaces, black-box test practice, statistics, archival summary systems, navigation, standards, or other bounded domains.

The target is not "compression exists." It is:

```text
small boundary representation
+
declared question family
→
exact task-sufficient behavior
```

### Kill condition

Reject cases where the compressed boundary appears sufficient only because difficult questions were silently omitted after the fact.

---

## BOUNTY 7 — REPEATED PROBE SIGNATURE

### Target shape

Find a case where one test was known to be ambiguous, but a small family of orthogonal tests produced a response signature that discriminated among candidate structures.

```text
R(W) = <response_I1, response_I2, response_I3>
```

The bounty should preserve how the probe family was chosen and what ambiguity remained after the full vector.

### Kill condition

Reject retrospective "test batteries" assembled only after the correct class was already known unless the historical record contains a genuine holdout/replay.
