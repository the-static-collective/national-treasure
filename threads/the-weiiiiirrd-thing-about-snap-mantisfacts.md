The WEIIIIIRRD thing about *snap*MANTISFACTS... is that the mantises were not really the center.

They were the knock on the wall.

We started with the fake-pop-science residue:

```text
mantis has weird eyes
mantis shrimp has twelve colors
praying mantis does spooky alien head stuff
```

Then the actual biology refused to stay flat.

The black spot in the praying mantis eye is not a pupil moving to follow the observer. It is an observer-dependent pseudopupil: the dark patch appears where ommatidia happen to align with the viewer.

Young mantises estimate distance by actively peering side to side, generating motion parallax through their own movement.

The praying mantis's ultrasonic ear is a single midline receiver: excellent for detecting bat-like ultrasonic attack structure, poor for ordinary left/right localization. Temporal cadence can carry action-relevant information without reconstructing a complete spatial world.

Mantis-shrimp eyes are stranger still. A single eye is physically divided into dorsal hemisphere, midband, and ventral hemisphere, with overlapping optical axes that can make one eye functionally trinocular. The animal actively rotates and scans the receiver. In polarization vision, physical optical structures preprocess the carrier before the nervous system receives it.

And then the famous flattened fact—

> mantis shrimps have twelve color receptors

—opened.

For the six-row stomatopod eye architecture under discussion, the twelve spectral classes are not twelve arbitrary entries in a bag.

They preserve a formation grammar:

```text
4 color-specialized midband rows
×
3 spectrally differentiated tiers per row
=
12 spectral classes
```

So:

```math
12 = 4 \times 3
```

But that was not interesting because `12` can be factored into `4 × 3`.

It was interesting because the anatomy independently tells us **which four triples are real**.

The twelve have addresses.

They are closer to:

```math
x_{r,t},\qquad r\in\{1,2,3,4\},\ t\in\{1,2,3\}
```

than to a flattened list:

```math
x_1,\ldots,x_{12}.
```

The count is not the receipt.

The relations that generated the count are the receipt.

## The question ring appeared

Then we stopped asking what the numbers meant and asked what **questions themselves require**.

A provisional primitive triad emerged:

```text
WHAT
WHICH
HOW
```

### WHAT — meaning / intelligibility under a decoder

Even apparently simple identification is relational.

```math
\operatorname{WHAT}(x\mid D,C)
```

means something like:

> What does `x` count as under decoder `D` in context `C`?

The noun is not stapled to the carrier.

A thing being identified as a mantis, manuscript, symbol, self, zero, receptor, or reason already assumes a grammar of distinction.

### WHICH — differentiation inside a candidate field

```math
\operatorname{WHICH}(S\mid C)
```

requires candidates and a discriminator.

From WHICH, several familiar question words can be viewed as specializations:

```text
WHO   = WHICH over agents / identities
WHERE = WHICH over positions relative to a frame
WHEN  = WHICH over temporal or causal ordering
```

This is not a claim about historical linguistics.

It is a compositional grammar.

### HOW — the DO word

HOW asks for an operation:

```math
x\xrightarrow{\text{HOW}}y.
```

HOW is transformation made interrogative.

### WHY — reason for a doing

WHY seemed less like a primitive peer and more like a typed interrogation of a HOW:

```math
\operatorname{WHY}_{R}(h)
```

where reason-type `R` must remain explicit:

```text
cause
motive
purpose
function
justification
```

The mantis ear provides the brutal specimen here.

Its current usefulness against bats does not automatically tell us the reason the auditory organ originated. Current function and formation cause are different WHYs.

## Counterevidence / brakes

1. `3`, `4`, `7`, and `12` appearing in multiple structures does not establish one hidden universal grammar.
2. The interrogative decomposition `WHAT / WHICH / HOW` → `WHO / WHERE / WHEN / WHY` is a designed analytical grammar, not demonstrated linguistic ancestry.
3. `TRINARY ENCLOSURE ⇒ QUATERNARY PROCESS` is a useful hypothesis, not a biological law established by mantis research.
4. Hall's symbolic method is not evidence that the symbols preserve the ancient lineage or metaphysical truths he attributes to them.
5. A static artifact may underdetermine its own history. Many formation paths can sometimes produce the same surviving state.
6. Memory is therefore not perfect reverse execution. It is preserved constraint on possible formation histories.
7. A reality-model generated recursively from questions remains a model; increasing articulation does not itself guarantee correspondence to external reality.
