Try the reading interface. Tell us what is unclear.

This pilot tests the interface and wording of the questions. It does not collect comprehension answers or contribute to a translation comparison.

Before you begin

Participation is voluntary. You can stop by closing this page before downloading any feedback. You do not have to answer any question or explain why you stopped.

The page runs locally and does not automatically transmit your responses. A feedback file downloads to your device only when you choose to create one; you decide whether to share it with the presenter. Your local files remain until you delete them. The presenter should delete any feedback you voluntarily share within seven days. Do not include your name, contact details, health information, or other personal information in comments.

You will see one passage and a comprehension question, but your answer to that question is not collected. Only usability feedback is exported. Version labels are masked, but you might recognize a translation from its language.
